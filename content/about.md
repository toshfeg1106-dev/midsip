# About

Hi — I'm Toshika.

Mid-Sip is my public brain. A living knowledge base that grows every time I go down a rabbit hole, finish a course, read a paper, or just notice something worth writing down.

## How it started

Sometime last year I began transitioning into a more intentional way of living, gently guided by herbs. What started as quiet curiosity slowly grew into practice — and then into a need to actually understand what I was doing and why.

I started keeping notes. Then the notes started connecting. Then I realised the connections were the interesting part.

This site is those connections, made visible.

## What you'll find here

- **Herb deep-dives** — what the science says, what tradition says, what I've actually noticed
- **Intentional living** — the practice of paying closer attention to ordinary things
- **Research notes** — things I'm mid-sip on right now, still forming
- **More chapters coming** — this is chapter one of many

## The newsletter

[[Still in Draft]] is my Substack — longer essays, personal essays, the messier thinking. This KB is the research layer underneath it.

If you want the writing, go there.  
If you want the web of ideas, stay here.
